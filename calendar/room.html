Axiom video room

Peer-to-peer, for a 1:1 lesson or a very small group. Whoever opens the room first is the host, and that tab has to stay open. This is not Google Meet.

Screen share isn’t available on iPhone or iPad. Camera, mic, and chat still work.

Network relay (TURN)

Most home networks connect with the public STUN servers. A school or mobile network sometimes blocks that. Paste a TURN relay if a call won’t connect. Metered Open Relay has a free tier. The setting stays in this browser.
